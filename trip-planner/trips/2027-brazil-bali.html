Safety, in short

Everywhere on this route is well-touristed: the family home, Teresópolis, Zona Sul in Rio (Copacabana/Ipanema), and Búzios, one of the calmer, more affluent beach towns in the state. Standard rules apply in Rio itself — stay in Zona Sul, use Uber after dark, keep phones out of sight on the beach. Rio's homicide rate has fallen two years running and the tourist strip added a large police patrol expansion in May 2026.
